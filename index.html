CYBERSHIELD SAFETY CENTER

Small steps. More peace of mind.

Not sure about a link?

Don’t open it. Avoid pasting sensitive or suspicious links into tools you don’t trust. Visit the organization’s official website by typing its address yourself, or contact them using a number you already know.

Worried about an account?

From a trusted device, go directly to the official service and change your password. Sign out other sessions if that option is available, turn on two-step verification, and contact the service through its official help page.

Lost money or shared personal information?

Contact your bank or payment provider using the number on your card or their official website. If you’re in immediate danger, contact local emergency services. Report online fraud to the appropriate authority in your country.

CyberShield is an educational demo. It does not scan links, monitor devices, or provide emergency response.
